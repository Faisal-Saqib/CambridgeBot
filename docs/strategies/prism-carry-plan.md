# Prism carry plan (proposed map experiment)

Status: proposed, not implemented or live. Current live v24 is described in [guarded-local.md](guarded-local.md). This plan incorporates the user's health, bodyguard and escape suggestions.

## Power and shopping

Prism gains 1.5 Focus per carried gem. Forty gems add 60 Focus: base Focus 80 becomes 140 before items, giving 210 damage from the 1.5-Focus spell before mitigation. Health helps Prism survive with this bonus; health itself does not increase Prism's Focus.

- Buy one Health-oriented Home emblem for health and a shorter recall. It reduces recall from five owner turn ends to three; duplicate Home passives do not stack.
- Follow with health-oriented upgrades and enough Focus for useful damage after tributing. Keep the gem reserve capped; 40 gems is an illustrative test point, not a validated optimum.
- Fund escorts' basic survivability before concentrating later camp rewards on Prism. Where safe, allies can clear a camp and stand outside its capture tiles while Prism receives the reward; abandon this arrangement when it delays a contested capture or exposes Prism.
- Stock one teleport crystal at spawn for a useful terrain escape. It costs 10 gold and only one may be carried. Prism must shop on spawn or while dead.

## Formation and protection

Keep Prism a few tiles behind the frontline, with healthy escorts close enough to help immediately. Ryth is a candidate frontline protector: he has an area stun and gains Strength equal to 1% of bonus health. A healing/support champion is another candidate. Exact picks and item ratios need testing on the selected maps.

Escorts need health too. Use positioning, stuns and coordinated attacks to slow pursuers; opponents can choose to ignore an escort and attack Prism. Rotate an injured protector out while another covers. Prefer escorts carrying few gems for the dangerous covering role.

## Retreat and escape

1. Start withdrawing when the frontline is about to fail or Prism's projected survival is too short. Do this while protection remains, allowing time for the entire escape channel.
2. Break contact and reach cover. A Home-assisted recall takes three owner turn ends and returns Prism to spawn with her gems intact. Damage or stun interrupts recall; do not repeatedly restart it under the same incoming attacks.
3. A teleport crystal moves at most three tiles, including diagonals, after a three-owner-turn channel. It crosses intervening terrain but cannot land on a wall, hole, altar, enemy spawn or occupied tile. It is useful when the terrain crossing creates an escape route; it cannot send Prism home from anywhere. Damage does not cancel it, but enemy stuns and death do, and it cannot be cancelled manually. Prism must survive the channel at her original position.
4. Escorts cover until Prism actually reaches safety, including while she recalls or teleports. After arriving on spawn, Prism stays through turn end to heal fully while keeping her gems.
5. Surviving escorts disengage and recall from safety, then regroup. A last stand can be justified if it buys Prism's escape, but routine sacrifices feed the enemy 100 base gold plus the escort's carried gems and remove that champion for 60 rounds. Do not intentionally throw away healthy escorts after Prism is safe.

If a tribute would win, the reserve cap is reached, or the game is ending, secure the gems. Under pressure, choose the safest achievable action: retreat/recall can preserve the gem-powered Focus; a reachable, unlocked altar may secure the bag. Tribute takes ceil(gems/10) owner turn ends and damage does not cancel it, but stun, death or a new altar lock can. Do not override escape with an unsafe banking detour.

## Changes required before implementation

V24 is only a starting point. Its generic support leader can be replaced by a banking ally, and recovering champions are excluded from leadership. A Prism mode must keep the protected-carry assignment through recall/teleport until arrival is confirmed, preserve emergency combat responses, and coordinate capture allocation, item purchases and the capped reserve. It must also avoid the rigid formation and shared-healing behavior that failed the previous whole-team experiment.

## Evaluation rules

First complete and analyze at least five v24 online games. Then test the isolated Prism candidate on contrasting maps and both sides before an online trial. Every new unranked request must target a currently top-20 team, rechecked before creation. Record wins, submitted gems, Prism's damage, bag losses, frontline deaths, completed/interrupted escape channels and time spent farming versus escorting. No improvement or successful escape is claimed yet.
