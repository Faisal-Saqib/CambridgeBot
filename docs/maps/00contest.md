- Primary: copy the most-frequent winning draft here — **Heart of Ruin, Marcella, Ryth, Vendetta, Vesper Vale** [measured].

### Camp targets

- One group holds ~100% of the map's 400 gems [derived]. That group IS the map: send the whole team at round 0, clear it together, escort the carrier to the altar. Conceding it = conceding the game (our the_line record: 0 points in 25 straight games [measured]).

- 50% of gems sit in one top region [derived] — treat it as the jackpot objective even if the map is otherwise spread out.

- Consumables: Altar Lock on the altar nearest our farm (top teams averaged 7.9 consumables/game here); TP crystal(s) for carrier escapes.

### Traversal / positioning

- Move as one group; nobody farms alone beyond ~6 tiles from the nearest ally. Escort carriers to the altar; fight only with 3+ bodies present.
- Bank at the nearest of 2 altars; tribute when a carrier holds ~200 gems or when an enemy is chasing — losing carried gems is the main way games here are lost.
