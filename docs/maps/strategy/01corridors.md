## Recommended plan

### Draft

- Primary: copy the most-frequent winning draft here — **Heart of Ruin, Marcella, Ryth, Vendetta, Vesper Vale** [measured].

### Camp targets

- One group holds ~57% of the map's 752 gems [derived]. That group IS the map: send the whole team at round 0, clear it together, escort the carrier to the altar. Conceding it = conceding the game (our the_line record: 0 points in 25 straight games [measured]).
- After the jackpot, farm outward in a cluster — do not spread solo while enemies are alive [hypothesis].
- 57% of gems sit in one top region [derived] — treat it as the jackpot objective even if the map is otherwise spread out.
- **Gated altar warning**: 57% of gems are monolith-held and only 2 altar(s) exist [derived]. Do NOT hold gems waiting for a group gate assault — bank through open altars early; if all altars are gated, break the gate around round 400-700 like top teams (not 900+), then tribute 200+ at once [measured].
